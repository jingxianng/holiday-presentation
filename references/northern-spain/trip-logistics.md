# Family Trip Logistics — Madrid → Oviedo → San Sebastián → Bilbao

**Dates:** Sat Sep 4 – Fri Sep 17, 2027 (X & E: 13 nights). Parents: Spain Sat Sep 4 – Wed Sep 15 (11 nights), bookended by Frankfurt.
**Season:** Spanish school holidays end Sep 1, so crowds drain out as we arrive; the sea is at its annual warmest; the driest window "Green Spain" offers (it's green because it rains — pack a layer and a light rain jacket). Madrid may still hit ~30°C; the trip cools as it goes north.
**Travelers:** Parents (Singapore → Frankfurt Thu Sep 2, down to Madrid Sat Sep 4) + X and partner (from the US East Coast).
**Shape:** 4 bases — Madrid 2 → Oviedo 4 → San Sebastián 3 → Bilbao (2 with parents + 2 couple's coda). Nobody backtracks; the group splits once, at a 20-minute airport run.
**Core principle:** No car until Oviedo. Madrid on foot and taxi; the fast train does the kilometers under the mountains; one one-way rental Oviedo → Bilbao airport, with the two driving transfers doubling as sightseeing days (caves + Santoña; Gernika + Bermeo + Gaztelugatxe).

## Leg 0 — Flights

- **Inbound, X & E:** EWR → MAD nonstop, lands ~13:20 Sat Sep 4. Taxi to the hotel.
- **Inbound, parents:** SIN → FRA Thu Sep 2 (arrive early evening same day); **FRA → MAD Sat Sep 4** — pick a late-morning departure so everyone converges by early evening.
- **Outbound, parents:** **BIO → FRA Wed Sep 15** (Lufthansa — ⚠️ verify the 2027 Wednesday rotation times), **Frankfurt airport hotel** that night (Hilton/Sheraton inside the terminal complex — no shuttle), **SQ FRA → SIN Thu Sep 16 ~noon**. Book BIO–FRA on a flexible fare — it's a self-transfer onto SQ, and the overnight is the buffer. Confirm LH and SQ terminals at FRA (both expected T1).
- **Outbound, X & E:** **BIO → MAD → EWR Fri Sep 17**, mid-afternoon departure. MAD–EWR is the Iberia business award. ⚠️ **Try to add BIO–MAD inside the award PNR** (Iberia awards can include a domestic feeder — protected connection, bags through). If stuck on separate tickets: Iberia for the hop, ≥3 h at MAD, carry-on-friendly bags.

## Leg 1 — Madrid (2 nights, no car)

- MAD → hotel: taxi (**fixed flat fare to the center — verify current rate, ~€33–35**).
- Base in the Sol–Huertas–La Latina–Retiro zone — walkable to Cava Baja, the Rastro, and the Prado.
- **Sat:** arrival, Cava Baja evening. **Sun:** Rastro + vermouth, Retiro, optional short Prado hop (timed ticket only if we decide to go — buyable same-day in September at off-peak hours, but verify), **Botín dinner (book ahead)**.

## Leg 2 — Madrid → Oviedo (Mon Sep 6)

- **Default:** Alvia from **Chamartín** (⚠️ not Atocha), ~3h15 via the Pajares base tunnel — pick a fast service, not a ~3h50 stopping one.
- **Segovia stopover option:** Segovia-Guiomar sits on the Madrid–Asturias high-speed line, and *some* Asturias-bound services stop there — others don't, and at least one traveler report says the morning Alvia can't be boarded at Segovia. ⚠️ **Unverified for 2027: check the Renfe timetable for an afternoon Segovia-Guiomar → Oviedo service when sales open** (search from "Segovia Guiomar" to "Oviedo"; if the Renfe site misbehaves, Trainline/Omio show the through services).
  - **If it exists:** morning private driver Madrid → Segovia (~1h, luggage rides in the car — ⚠️ get a quote; the day's cost driver), 4–5 h in town, driver drops everyone + bags at Guiomar (~7 km out), afternoon train, **arrive Oviedo early evening**.
  - **Fallback if no workable afternoon train:** Avant/AVE Chamartín → Segovia with luggage stored in town (⚠️ verify a left-luggage option near the aqueduct), back to Guiomar, change onto the Oviedo service — more handling, less recommended with parents. Or skip Segovia; nothing downstream changes.
- Buy seats when Renfe sales open (released in waves, typically a few months out — set a reminder).
- Oviedo station is ~10 min on foot from the old town. **Pick up the rental Tuesday morning** (no car needed Monday evening; saves a night of parking).

## Leg 3 — Oviedo hub (4 nights, Mon–Fri)

- Base in/adjacent to the old town, **with parking**; the trip's nicest hotel is justified here (the Reconquista is the grande-dame — verify current state before booking).
- **One-way rental: Oviedo → Bilbao airport** — ⚠️ confirm the one-way fee (typically €50–150 intra-Spain), automatic if needed, Tuesday-morning branch hours, and **Wednesday Sep 15 drop at BIO** (see Leg 6).
- Three full days, **ordered by weather**:
  1. **Covadonga (clearest forecast):** Cangas de Onís (~1h05), ⚠️ **Alsa shuttle to the Lakes — road closed to private cars in season; book online; the season's plan publishes ~early spring 2027, set a reminder.** Bad morning = float the day.
  2. **West coast:** Cudillero caldereta lunch (+ Luarca). No bookings.
  3. **City + spa:** Oviedo on foot, Naranco by taxi, **Las Caldas** afternoon (check day-visit booking).
- Thursday evening: pack for an ~08:30 Friday departure.

## Leg 4 — Transfer: Oviedo → San Sebastián via the caves (Fri Sep 10)

- ~1h45 to **Puente Viesgo** → ⚠️ **El Castillo caves, timed late-morning slot (~11:00–11:30); groups capped at about a dozen — book as soon as sales open for the dates.** Fallback if unavailable: Tito Bustillo (Ribadesella) as an Oviedo bench-day visit, also book-ahead.
- ~40 min to **Santoña** for lunch (anchovy shops).
- ~1h45 to San Sebastián; arrive late afternoon.
- Luggage loaded once in Oviedo; trunk shut until SS; valuables/meds/passports in daypacks (the car sits at a cave car park and a harbor for hours — don't leave anything visible).

## Leg 5 — San Sebastián (3 nights, Fri–Mon)

- **Hotel with parking, Parte Vieja-adjacent** — SS parking is the tightest of the trip; confirm at booking. Car untouched except for the Getaria outing.
- **Elkano: book the moment reservations open, for Saturday or Sunday lunch** — ⚠️ verify its weekly closing days before choosing; Kaia Kaipe as fallback. Getaria is ~25–30 min west; Zumaia on the way back.
- Other day: Pasaia, Hondarribia, or the beach — no bookings.

## Leg 6 — Transfer: San Sebastián → Bilbao via Urdaibai (Mon Sep 13)

- **Gernika** (~1h; Monday market day) → **Bermeo** lunch (20 min) → **Gaztelugatxe** (10 min; viewpoint free, ⚠️ **free timed permit required to walk down in season — book if doing the steps**) → **Bilbao** (~40 min).
- ⚠️ **Guggenheim: traditionally closed Mondays outside high summer — irrelevant now (Monday is the transfer), but confirm Tuesday Sep 14 hours.**
- **Car: drop it at BIO on Wednesday morning when taking the parents** (drive them, return the car, taxi back ~20 min). Nothing in the couple's coda needs a car, and it saves two nights of Bilbao parking. Book the rental's return for Wed Sep 15 at BIO accordingly.

## Leg 7 — Bilbao (Mon–Fri)

- **Hotel with parking for Mon–Tue** (Casco Viejo side for atmosphere; confirm garage access — the old town is pedestrianized). Parents' room Mon–Wed only.
- **Tue:** Guggenheim (timed entry), Ribera market, Artxanda funicular; farewell dinner.
- **Wed:** parents to BIO in the rental car, car returned there; parents' LH to Frankfurt.
- **Wed–Thu:** couple's coda — Algorta/Santurtzi + Vizcaya Bridge by metro, late pintxos, Guggenheim interior properly if wanted.
- **Fri Sep 17:** taxi to BIO (15–25 min, ~€25–30) for the mid-afternoon chain.

## Catches & open questions

- **Segovia stopover is unverified** — it hinges on an afternoon Segovia → Oviedo train existing in the 2027 timetable and on a sane luggage plan. Nothing else depends on it.
- **Parents' Wednesday** is a two-leg day (BIO–FRA, then the terminal hotel) — gentle, but book the LH leg with enough slack that a delay doesn't matter; the overnight already protects the SQ flight.
- **Friday transfer is the long day** (~3h45 driving + caves + lunch). If the cave slot lands too early or late to make it comfortable, drop the caves and take the Tito Bustillo fallback from Oviedo instead.
- **Elkano closing days** decide whether Getaria is Saturday or Sunday.
- All hours, fares, and schedules above are from memory unless noted — verify at booking.
